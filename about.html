About Us!

The two students who created this webpage are two freshman students studying Game Design at Purdue University. Wanting to implement both elements of games and website-designing, we decided to combine both skills into one big project, thus creating this Minecraft-themed website. In this website, we used HTML coding as well as CSS decorating, studied the mechanics and distinct levels of Minecraft, and researched how using a video game can attract a specific group of people to view and look at our website. This website was made through an assignment in our class called CGT 141, where we were assigned to make a fully-functional website tailored to your interests and hobbies. Through it all, this project has taught us many HTML and CSS skills, but also opened new ideas and perspectives in us to make for the future.
