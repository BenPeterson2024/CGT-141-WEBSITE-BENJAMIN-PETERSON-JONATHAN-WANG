Spawn

Welcome to the game of Minecraft! When creating a Minecraft world, the world uses a generation system using whats called a seed. This is a series of numbers to determine how to generate the world. You can even enter your own seed! However, the chance that someone who randomly generates a world gets the same seed as another person, is astronomically low.
It's 1 in 1,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 to be exact! So it's safe to say every world is unique!

Let's create your first world! When you first open the game, your main page will look something like below. Depending upon your current version, it may look different, but it should follow the same layout.

Go ahead and click the "Singleplayer" button. The following page is where you can see your current worlds, as well as creating new ones. Assuming this is your first time playing, go ahead and select the "Create New World" button.

This guide is based on how to beat the game in the survival or hardcore game modes. The default settings should be perfect for a new player. Hardcore mode is strongly advised for only seasoned players, as once you die, you cannot respawn. Go ahead and name your world in the text bar. Make sure "Allow Commands" is set to off. You may choose whatever difficulty you want, but the game is unbeatable in peaceful difficulty. Due to this, this guide does not work for peaceful difficulty.

Once you're ready, go ahead and click "Create New World" to generate the world!

Once your world is finished generating, you'll notice a few things. You have nine cells in the bottom of the screen. These cells are your hot bar. These are the items you can select by using either your scroll wheel, or the numbers on the top of your keyboard. Your number pad will not work.

Go ahead and hit the E button on your keyboard. This opens your inventory. You'll notice several more cellss than what was in your hotbar. The nine separated cells is your hotbar from before. This is located beneath the large block of cells above it. This allows you to hold more items than what is in your hot bar. If you want something from your inventory in your hotbar and you have an open hotbar cell, simply just click your inventory item, move to your hotbar cell, and click again to place into your hot bar. If you want to change a hotbar item out, simply use your mouse to click the hotbar item into your inventory, and an item from your inventory in its place!

Find a nearby tree. It doesn't need to be the same kind I am at. Any tree will work!

To break the tree, walk up to it, aim your crosshair at the wood, and hold left click down. This will begin to break the tree. After a few seconds of holding down, the wood will break!

To collect your wood, walk into the small floating block of wood. Congrats! You've broken your first block! This is the first major step in your journey. Go ahead and chop down the rest of the tree.

Open your inventory. Drag your logs up into one of the four cells that says crafting.

You'll notice in the small cell off to the right side, that there is now planks. Go ahead and left click on those planks until you have no more wood. You can also hold shift, and click it once to craft the maximum amount of an item.

Now while holding the planks, go ahead and right click on every single cell in your crafting square. Then set the rest of the extra planks down in your inventory. If done properly, there should only be one piece of wood in each spot of the crafting square. You'll see that you have the option to create one crafting table. Go ahead and click on the crafting table.

Place the crafting table in your hotbar and close your inventory.

While looking at the ground, right click to place down the crafting table. Right click again on the crafting table to open it!

Now that you know crafting from the previous portion. We are going to craft a wooden pickaxe! In the crafting table place one piece of wood in the center and one beneath it. This will craft sticks. As you can see, not all recipes are one to one. The pair of planks gives you four sticks! Place your sticks in your inventory.

Next we will craft our pickaxe. Firstly, take three planks and place them all in the top row. Then take two sticks, placing one in the middle square, and the other in the bottom square.

Following this, we will craft a shovel. Place a single oak plank in the top middle square, and then place two more sticks in the same location as the pickaxe.

Make sure both the pickaxe and the shovel are in your hotbar.

Now you're ready to mine! Go ahead and equip your shovel. Shovels work best on blocks where you would use a shovel in real life! This includes but is not limited to, grass blocks, dirt blocks, sand blocks, gravel blocks, and many more!

Using your shovel, construct a staircase down until you reach a gray block. This block is stone!
NOTE! If you're in a desert, you'll first reach a block called sandstone. This looks similar to regular sand. You need your pickaxe to mine sandstone. If you continue to dig down, you will reach stone.
Your staircase needs to be about two blocks high in order for your character to be able to walk down.

Once you reach stone, you should switch to your pickaxe to mine it. Most of the hard earthy blocks in Minecraft require a pickaxe to be broken.

When you break a stone block, it gets turned into cobblestone. Go ahead and collect eighteen Cobblestone.

Once you have eighteen cobblestone, it's time for more crafting! Go ahead and return to your crafting table.

These wooden tools are not very useful and break very quickly, so it's time to upgrade! We will be making a new pickaxe and shovel. On top of that we will also be making an axe, a sword, and a furnace!

For the sword, go ahead and place a cobblestone in both the top center and dead center. Then place a stick in the bottom center.

For the axe, place a cobblestone in both the middle left, the top left, and the top center of the table. Then place a stick in the center and the bottom center.

For the furnace, go ahead and place a cobblestone in every block except the center!

Use the same crafting recipe as the wooden-pickaxe, and the wooden-shovel, but replace with cobblestone for the stone versions!

Using your new axe, go and chop down some trees!

After you have collected your wood, place your furnace down. Open the furnace and you'll notice 3 cells. The top cell is what you're cooking, the bottom cell is your fuel, and the cell on the right is your output. We need to make some torches, so lets test this out! Put some planks in the fuel cell. Then put some logs in the burn cell. Now you're cooking charcoal! While this is cooking go ahead and cut down some more trees or some mine some more cobblestone. You're going to need a lot of blocks here eventually!

Now we are going to craft some torches! Go ahead and place your sticks below your coal anywhere in the crafting table or your inventory crafting. This time, use just as many stick as you did coal! If you have too much of one, for example, sticks, the extra sticks will return to your inventory. Collect all of your torches. Coal can be used as a fuel in a furnace which is far better than planks or logs.

Torches are vital in minecraft, not only for sight, but for protection. When it gets dark, mobs begin to spawn. Mobs will be discussed momentarily. You can place your torches like you would place a block. You can place them on flat ground or on a wall. It's probably starting to get dark in your Minecraft world at this point. Now it's time to protect yourself! Let's go ahead and create a door before we build a shelter.

The crafting recipe for a door is quite simple. A door requires six planks. Leave three spots open from the top to the bottom on the right side of the crafting table. You now have three doors!

Go ahead and use you're creativity. Create a base to wait out the night. Don't forget to use your torches to light up the inside of your base and some of the area around it. Your base doesn't need to be as big as mine but here is an example! Do not include open holes for windows at this point. If you want windows, you can smelt sand inside of a furnace. This will give you glass for your windows to protect you from hostile Mobs, while allowing you to see outside!

Now that it's night, lets go over Mobs. Now what is a "Mob"? A Mob is simply a mobile entity. So any creature in Minecraft is a "Mob". However, there are three types of Mobs in Minecraft. Firstly, there are hostile Mobs. Hostile Mobs can only spawn in low light levels.
* Spawning means to begin existing in Minecraft.
There are some that break this rule, but for the most part, nearly all hostile Mobs follow this rule. This means that hostile Mobs can only be found at night, in caves, or other dark locations. Hostile Mobs will try to attack you and hurt you. That's why we built a shelter! Lets go over some of the common ones.

Firstly there are Zombies. Zombies have a fair bit of health and can see you from far away. However, they're very slow, except for the Baby Zombie. Baby Zombies can run really fast so you can't out run them, and they're very small so they're harder to hit.

Next we have Skeletons. Skeletons keep their distance and shoot you with a Bow and Arrow. Since they don't chase after you, or at least not quickly, they're relatively easy to run away from if you can't fight from a range.

Now we're onto spiders. The previous two Mobs burn to death in the daylight. Spiders do not burn in the daylight. They will however become a peaceful Mob during the day. Be wary though, they still attack when attacked themselves.

Then there are Creepers. Creepers are a silent, hostile, Mob. Hence the name. Creepers do not burn in daylight. Unlike spiders, they are still hostile during the day. Creepers are slower compared to other Mobs. They will explode once they get close to you, damaging the area around them, and can easily kill you if you don't have any armor. They will make a hissing sound prior to exploding. This is to give you a split second to react to try to save yourself. So always be aware of your surroundings day and night!

Enderman are the next hostile Mob you have to look out for. Enderman do not burn during the day. Enderman can pick up blocks and place them back down. They are a peacful Mob unless either attacked, or if you look directly at them. Don't look at them in the eyes! Enderman can teleport to a new location which causes the fight to be disorenting. Enderman deal a lot of damage so it's advised to not fight them until later in the game if possible.

There are many hostile Mobs in Minecraft. These are the most common ones which is all that will be covered in depth for this portion of the guide. There are also Boss Mobs which include The Ender Dragon, The Wither, The Elder Guardian, and The Warden. This guide is on how to beat the Ender Dragon.

Then there are the passive Mobs in minecraft. The following passive Mobs can be used for food. Cows, pigs, sheep, chickens, cod/salmon, rabbits, polarbears, and more! All of the food types that are dropped by these creatures, when killed, can be cooked in a furnace for better hunger replenishment compared to raw. Chicken can also cause the Hunger Effect if eaten raw. The Hunger Effect depleats your hunger bar for about 30 seconds. This effect can also be achieved by eating rotten flesh, which is dropped by Zombies when killed. You need to eat food to replenish your hunger bar. If your hunger bar gets down to three, then you can no longer sprint. If it gets to zero, you will begin to take damage.
